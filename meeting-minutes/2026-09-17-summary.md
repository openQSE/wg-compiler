Thanks to those who participated in our work session today on our survey
paper. Please continue to contribute to our survey paper draft & look at
how you can contribute you you have not yet started!

Please note that the survey paper is our 6-month goal and we are about 3
months into the time we have for it.

Also, we discussed a question from the QRI/QDMI/QRMI working group about
whether translation from one circuit format to another should happen
before or after circuit submission. Our consensus view is that we start
with the assumption that it happens as part of the SDK and SDK adapter's
work; and specific compilation steps that must happen close to circuit
submission time can still be performed when needed. The implication is
that QRI/QDMI/QRMI need to transmit circuits in the QPU-preferred
interchange formats.
